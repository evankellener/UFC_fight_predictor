How the model thinks.

A logistic regression on 207 engineered features — MMA-AI per-fight averages, Elo from two sources, recency, style, and weight-class history — trained symmetrically (each fight appears as both orientations) to eliminate red-corner bias. Recency-weighted training (λ=1.20/yr) and 2-year rolling weight-class baselines keep the model honest against era drift. Walk-forward accuracy 71.6% over 522 out-of-sample fights (2024-04 → 2026-04), with +14.0% ROI on +EV bets vs Vegas odds. Below: the architecture, the validation, and tools to interrogate any of it yourself.

03b

λ sweep — era drift

Training samples are weighted by exp(−λ × years_ago). Lower λ = treat old fights as nearly-equal to recent ones; higher λ = aggressively down-weight the past. If the optimal λ drifts with the fold, that's a fingerprint of era-specific feature drift. Best λ is monotonically increasing with fold recency — that's exactly what drift looks like.
